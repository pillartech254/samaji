Why Samaji

One platform, your whole school

Stop juggling spreadsheets, paper receipts and disconnected tools. Samaji brings every part of school operations together.

Fees & receipts that add up

Set fee structures per class, collect by cash, M-Pesa or bank, and print colourful professional receipts. Balances update instantly.

Complete student records

Full biodata, class & stream, boarding house, guardians and medical notes — pick a class at registration, no typing.

Insightful reports

Live dashboards and charts for enrollment, collections and attendance — know exactly where your school stands.

Secure & private

Each school's data is isolated with row-level security. Daily backups and 99.9% uptime keep you running.

Reach every parent

Send announcements and SMS to parents, teachers or the whole school — keep your community informed.
